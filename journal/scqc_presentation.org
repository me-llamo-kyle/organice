* Moral of the story
Mapping quantum evolution onto space curves enables understanding the entire solution space and reverse-engineering driving pulses with optimal properties. Space curve formalism starts from the quantum evolution and extracts driving pulses from it - no solving the Schrodinger equation required!

* SLIDE Overview of quantum optimal control
Finding control pulses to implement a given gate with certain optimal properties, like:
- Minimizing error
  - Quasistatic
  - Leakage
- Minimizing gate time
- Minimizing control bandwidth
* SLIDE Why does it matter?
- Faster & higher fidelity physical gates make QEC less expensive
- Minimized control bandwidth makes implementation easier & higher fidelity
* SLIDE Infinite dimensional optimization problem
- A la calculus of variations
- Necessary: Pontryagin's maximum principle
- Sufficient: Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equation
- Not feasible for any real problems

* SLIDE Space Curve Quantum Control
- Flips the problem on its head
- Finding optimal evolution -> drawing a space curve with specific geometric properties
- "Backwards mode": geometry of the space curve encodes pulse shape information
* SLIDE Basic 2D example
** SLIDE H = (Delta + delta)/2 Z + Omega(t)/2 X
- Minimize quasistatic Z error delta
** SLIDE Interaction picture + magnus expansion = space curve
- Eliminating error to first order -> closed space curve
- Curve length = gate time
- Curvature = driving pulse
- Angle between initial and final tangent vector = gate rotation angle
- Drawing any closed curve -> first order optimal control solution!

- Evolving ZZ term -> space curve
